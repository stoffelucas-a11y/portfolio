## DESIGN DIRECTION FOR THE ONE-PAGER

**Aesthetic:** Dark, architectural, restrained. Must feel like it could exist in Demon's world without looking like a copy of their website.

**Tone:** Cold but human. Industrial but considered. NOT sleek startup, NOT fashion blogger, NOT agency portfolio.

**Typography:** Condensed or extended grotesque. Something with character — not Inter, not Space Grotesk, nothing generic. Consider something with an Italian or industrial flavour. Monospace accents work well for secondary text (coordinates, labels, codes — like Demon's own visual language with "EST 1963", "31044", "FIELD UNIT 01").

**Color:** Near-black background. Off-white or warm grey text. One accent — deep red or rust (referencing Demon's own red-on-black visual language from their intern post) OR raw concrete grey. Do not use both.

**Layout:** Asymmetric. Grid-breaking where it earns it. Generous negative space. No centred hero with a headshot. Think editorial spread, not CV.

**Textures:** Subtle grain or noise overlay on backgrounds. Concrete or fabric texture reference. Not heavy-handed.

**Motion:** Minimal but deliberate. Slow fade-in on load. Possibly a subtle parallax on the moodboard grid. Nothing that feels like a template animation.

**No:** rounded corners, soft gradients, pastel anything, icons with drop shadows, skill bars, timeline CVs, "hire me" CTAs.

## CRITICAL WARNINGS

1. **Do not over-explain.** Alberto Deon will read negative space. Leave room for curiosity.
2. **Do not use Demon's exact visual identity.** This should feel adjacent, not imitative.
3. **The moodboard is a demonstration of taste, not a mood board for a brief.** It should feel personal and specific, not aggregated.
4. **Short is better.** This person is applying to a one-man brand. A 12-scroll portfolio page is wrong. This should load, land, and leave an impression in under 2 minutes.
5. **The work section must be honest.** If there are only two pieces worth showing, show two pieces confidently rather than five with apologies.
6. **Mobile must work.** Alberto is almost certainly reading this on his phone.
