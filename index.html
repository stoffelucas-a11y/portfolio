W — 02

AI Conversation Coach

Ran for years without a coach — borrowed plans from friends, followed generic schedules, had AI generate training blocks that never quite fit. What was always missing was something that moves with life the way a real coach does. After moving back from Thailand and a long break from training, I got a bib for the 20km of Brussels — one of the most iconic races in the city. Sub 1:20 felt like a realistic comeback goal, but it needed proper structure and something to stay accountable to. So I built this. Entirely by hand, no template, no shortcut. A conversational coach that adapts when life shifts, adjusts the plan when a week goes sideways, and keeps building toward race day. More useful than expected — for structure, for motivation, and quietly, for confidence.

02 MOODBOARD

Curated to show how I see, not what I know.
